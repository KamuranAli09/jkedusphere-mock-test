Where the marks are

40 of the 100 questions are technical and the other 60 are aptitude: English 15, GK India 15, GK J&K 5, Numerical and Reasoning 15 and Computers 10. The aptitude syllabus is the same as the Executive constable paper, with fewer questions per subject.

The technical syllabus has four areas: Radio Communication, Television Transmission, Electronics and Telecommunication, and Electricity. The notice does not say how the 40 questions are split between them, so treat all four as fair game.

6. Technical Syllabus40 questions

Radio Communication

  • Modulation: amplitude and frequency modulation, modulation index, carrier signal, modulated signal
  • AM modulator, FM modulator, demodulation, bandwidth, AM and FM applications
  • AM receiver (superheterodyne radio receiver) and FM receiver
  • Radio wave propagation: ground wave, sky wave, space wave; AF, IF, RF

Television Transmission

  • Television transmitters
  • Antennas: types, dipole antenna, transmission line feeders, coaxial cables
  • Persistence of vision, picture transmission, scanning in the picture tube
  • Progressive and interlaced scanning, composite video signal, negative picture pulses
  • Video signal bandwidth, channel bandwidth, SSB (single side band) transmission, VHF and UHF ranges

Electronics and Telecommunication

  • Radio wave propagation: principle, types of propagation, fading
  • Modulation and demodulation: need for modulation, types, AM, FM and PM, SSB-SC and DSB-SC
  • Block diagram of AM and FM transmitter, FM generation and detection, AM, FM and PM comparison
  • Antenna: fundamentals, parameters, types and applications
  • Digital modulation and demodulation: techniques, sampling, quantization and encoding, multiplexing and de-multiplexing, PAM / PPM / PWM signals and principles
  • Mobile communication: basics, cell site, hand off, frequency reuse, block diagram and working of cell phones, cell phone features, GSM and CDMA technology
  • Communication equipment: AM and FM radio concept and working, television concept and working, optical fibre equipment
  • Electrical and electronic gadgets: UPS, inverter, stabilizer and SMPS working
  • e-Governance: objectives, origins in India, e-governance projects in India, work plan and infrastructure, DBMS, anti-virus

Electricity

  • Electrical units, effects of electric current, conductors and insulators, types of solder and flux, AC circuits
  • Magnetic terms and units, magnetic materials, properties of a magnet, laws of electromagnetism
  • Wiring, IE rules, wiring accessories: switches, fuses, MCB, ELCB, MCCB, RCCB, relays; earthing: megger, earth tester
  • DC generator: principle, parts and functions, types, EMF equation, voltage build-up
  • Three phase induction motor: principle, parts and functions, slip, speed, rotor frequency, torque, copper loss, applications
