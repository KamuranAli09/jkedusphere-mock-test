Where the marks are

In Prelims, Reasoning carries 40 of the 100 marks, more than English (30) or Quantitative Aptitude (30), even though it gets the same 20 minutes. Each section is timed separately, so a section you finish early doesn't give you extra time elsewhere.

In Mains, Reasoning, General / Economy / Banking Awareness and Data Analysis & Interpretation carry 60 marks each, which is 180 of the 200 objective marks. English is only 20 marks, but you also need it for the 25-mark descriptive paper, where banking and current affairs topics come up as essays.

Questions aspirants ask

What is the J&K Bank PO exam pattern?

There are three stages: an online Prelims, an online Mains and an interview. Prelims has 100 questions for 100 marks in 60 minutes, split into English (30 questions), Quantitative Aptitude (35) and Reasoning (35), with 20 minutes for each section. Mains has 170 objective questions for 200 marks in 160 minutes, plus a 25-mark descriptive paper of 2 questions in 30 minutes.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. One quarter of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer in both Prelims and Mains objective tests. Unattempted questions carry no penalty.

Does the Prelims count towards the final merit?

No. Prelims is there to shortlist candidates for Mains. Final merit is worked out from Mains and the interview, with Mains carrying 80% and the interview 20%.

What does the Mains syllabus include that Prelims doesn't?

Mains adds General / Economy / Banking Awareness with Digital and Financial Awareness, a full Data Analysis & Interpretation section and a descriptive paper with an essay and comprehension. Reasoning and English also get harder than in Prelims.

Which syllabus is the series based on?

The pattern and topics J&K Bank published for its 2026 Probationary Officer recruitment (notification JKB/HR/Rectt/2026-492, dated 7 November 2026). Confirm the final details in the official notification, and watch for exam dates, which the bank announces separately.

What's in the pack?

Prelims and Mains mock tests, sectional practice, Practice Mode, personalised performance analysis, and a live leaderboard with rank tracking.

What is Practice Mode?

Practice Mode has no timer and shows the answer as soon as you respond, so it works well while you're still learning a topic. Practice attempts aren't ranked. Exam Mode is timed, has negative marking and counts towards the leaderboard.

What does the performance analysis show?

After a test you see your score and a breakdown by section. Over several attempts it shows which sections are strongest and which keep costing you marks, so you know what to work on next.
