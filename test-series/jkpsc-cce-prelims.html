Prelims syllabus

Our tests are mapped to the JKPSC prelims syllabus. Here is what each paper covers.

Paper I: General Studies (200 marks)
  • Current events of national and international importance
  • History of India and the Indian National Movement
  • Indian and World Geography
    • Physical geography
    • Social geography
    • Economic geography of India and the world
  • Indian Polity and Governance
    • Constitution
    • Political system
    • Panchayati Raj
    • Public policy
    • Rights issues
  • Economic and Social Development
    • Sustainable development
    • Poverty
    • Inclusion
    • Demographics
    • Social sector initiatives
  • General issues on environmental ecology, biodiversity and climate change that do not require subject specialisation
  • General Science

Questions about Jammu & Kashmir may also be asked in this paper.

Paper II: CSAT (200 marks, qualifying)
  • Comprehension
  • Interpersonal skills including communication skills
  • Logical reasoning and analytical ability
  • Decision making and problem solving
  • General mental ability
  • Basic numeracy (numbers and their relations, orders of magnitude, etc.), Class X level
  • Data interpretation (charts, graphs, tables, data sufficiency, etc.), Class X level

Paper II is a qualifying paper with the minimum qualifying marks fixed at 33%. Candidates must appear in both papers.

How we'd suggest using it

Start with the previous papers. Sit a couple of years of GS Paper I in Exam Mode before you study anything new. Your score doesn't matter yet. What you're after is a feel for how the commission frames its questions and which subjects you're weakest in.

Then go subject by subject. Use the topic-wise tests in Practice Mode while you're still reading a topic, and switch to Exam Mode once you've revised it.

Give CSAT regular time. It's only qualifying, but you do need 33% in it. Doing a CSAT test every few days is far easier than cramming it in the last month.

Keep the full mocks for the final stretch, and read your performance report after each one. The report tells you where the marks went, which is more useful than the score itself.

Questions aspirants ask

What is the JKPSC CCE prelims exam pattern?

Prelims has two objective papers, General Studies Paper I and General Studies Paper II (CSAT). Each carries 200 marks and gets 2 hours, and each has 100 questions in recent years. Both are in English, and one third of the marks for a question is deducted for every wrong answer. You have to appear in both papers.

Is CSAT only qualifying?

Yes. Paper II (CSAT) is a qualifying paper and you need at least 33% in it. Paper I marks are what count for the prelims cut-off. Even so, you cannot skip CSAT, because falling short of 33% ends your attempt.

How many years of previous papers are included?

10+ years of previous question papers, and CSAT is part of that. You can attempt them like a test, with a timer and a rank, or in Practice Mode.

Are there subject-wise and topic-wise tests?

Yes. General Studies is covered subject by subject and topic by topic, so you can test yourself on History, Polity, Geography, Economy, Environment, Science and Current Events as you finish each one, rather than waiting for a full mock.

How many CSAT tests are there?

20+ CSAT tests, along with PYQ practice. They cover comprehension, logical reasoning and analytical ability, decision making, general mental ability, and basic numeracy and data interpretation, which is what the syllabus lists for Paper II.
