Why previous year papers are worth your time

The topics and question style for a given post tend to stay similar from one recruitment cycle to the next. Two or three past papers will tell you more about what the board expects than a week of guessing.

They also show you where you really stand. A past paper is closer to the board's own style than a question set someone wrote to imitate it, so your score on one is a more honest starting point.

And they train your timing. Finishing a full paper in the allotted time is a skill of its own, and the only way to build it is to sit papers under a clock.

How we'd suggest using them

Sit one paper cold. Pick the paper for the post you're applying to and take it in Exam Mode before you revise anything. The score doesn't matter yet. You're after a list of weak subjects.

Read the report, then revise. Your breakdown shows which subjects gave you marks and which cost you. Spend the next week on the ones that cost you.

Retake in Practice Mode. Go through the same paper again with no timer, so you can see the right answer for each question you missed and why.

Come back to Exam Mode. A week or two later, sit a different paper and compare. If the post you want has only one paper here, use one of a similar level for the general sections.

Questions aspirants ask

Are the JKSSB PYQs really free?

Yes. You only need a free JKEdusphere account. There's nothing to buy to attempt the papers, and no card details are asked for. Premium mock test series are separate and optional.

Which JKSSB papers are available right now?

Papers from recent JKSSB exams, including Constable (Executive, Armed, IRP, SDRF), Constable Telecom, Constable Photographer, Finance Account Assistant, Junior Assistant, Website Operator, Health Educator, Nursing Aid, Inspector FPF and more. The list on this page shows what's there today.

Will more papers be added?

Yes. We add papers as new exams happen and as we get hold of older ones. We announce each new paper on our Telegram channel, t.me/JKEdusphere, so that's the quickest way to know.

My post isn't on the list. What should I do?

Tell us on Telegram which paper you want and we'll try to add it. In the meantime, a paper of a similar level is still useful for the general sections like English, GK and reasoning.

What is the difference between Practice Mode and Exam Mode?

Practice Mode has no timer and shows the answer as soon as you respond. It isn't ranked. Exam Mode is timed and counts towards the leaderboard, so it's the one to use when you want to know where you really stand.

Can I attempt a paper more than once?

Yes, as many times as you like. Your best Exam Mode attempt is the one tracked on the leaderboard.

What do I see after finishing a paper?

Your score and a breakdown by subject, so you can see which sections gave you marks and which cost you.

How are PYQs different from mock tests?

A PYQ is a paper from an earlier exam, so it shows you how the board has framed questions for that post. A mock test is written fresh to mimic the pattern. Use PYQs to learn the style and mocks to practise at volume.

Where do I find the latest updates?

On our Telegram channel, t.me/JKEdusphere, along with new test alerts and exam notices.
