Questions before you buy

How do I receive the PDF after payment?

Right after you pay, our server checks the payment with Razorpay and your download starts automatically. If it doesn't, tap the download button on the confirmation screen. The link works only once, so save the PDF to Google Drive or your phone straight away.

Which payment methods are accepted?

UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), debit cards, credit cards, net banking and wallets, all through Razorpay.

Is my payment safe?

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We only ask Razorpay to confirm that your payment went through before we release the file.

Do I need an account to buy a note?

No. You can buy a note without signing up, and your download appears on the same page as soon as the payment is verified.

I paid, but the download didn't start. What should I do?

First tap the download button on the confirmation screen, because some phones block automatic downloads. If you closed the page or saw an error, message us on WhatsApp with your Razorpay Payment ID (it starts with pay_). We resolve it within 24 hours.

Can I download the PDF again later?

The download link works once and expires after 15 minutes, so save the file as soon as you get it. If you lose it, message us on WhatsApp with your Payment ID and we will look into it.

What is the difference between a single note and a bundle?

A bundle gives you several PDFs for one payment and usually costs less than buying them one by one. The page shows exactly which PDFs are inside and how much you save. After payment, the files download one after another.

Which notes should I buy for my exam?

Tap your exam at the top of the page to see only its notes. Read the Includes line on each note: it lists what the PDF covers and, where it applies, the marks it carries in the paper. If you are starting the JKPSI Telecom syllabus from scratch, the bundle is usually the cheapest way to cover all of it.

Can I read the notes on my phone?

Yes. They are standard PDF files that open on any phone, tablet or computer, and once downloaded you can read them offline.

Are the notes updated for 2026 exams?

Yes. All notes follow the latest UPSC, JKPSC and JKSSB patterns, with content up to March 2026.
