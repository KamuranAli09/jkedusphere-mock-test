Detailed syllabus, topic by topic

Our tests are built around these exact topics, so nothing in the notice is left out and nothing outside it eats your time. Tap a subject to see what's in it.

Part A General Awareness, Aptitude & Communication

1. General Knowledge & Current Affairs20 marks
  • Major national and international events
  • Indian economy: budget, flagship schemes, reforms
  • Developments in science & technology
  • Space, defence and nuclear programmes
  • Indian Polity: Preamble, Fundamental Rights & Duties, Directive Principles of State Policy
  • Disaster Management, climatic change
2. General Knowledge with Special Reference to J&K UT10 marks
  • History of Jammu & Kashmir
  • Geography: rivers, lakes, climate, soils, flora & fauna
  • Jammu & Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019
3. General English & Communication Skills10 marks
  • Grammar: tenses, modals, articles, prepositions
  • Active & passive voice
  • Direct & indirect speech
  • Synonyms, antonyms, idioms and phrases
  • Reading comprehension
  • Error spotting and sentence improvement
  • Basics of professional communication: official drafting, email etiquette and technical terminology
4. General Intelligence & Reasoning10 marks
  • Analogies
  • Number and letter series
  • Coding–decoding
  • Venn diagrams
  • Syllogisms
  • Blood relations
  • Logical reasoning and pattern recognition
5. Basic Numeracy10 marks
  • Ratio and proportion
  • Percentages
  • Profit and loss
  • Simple and compound interest
  • Averages
  • Time, speed and distance
  • Elementary statistics and data interpretation

Part B Technical / Professional Knowledge

6. Engineering Mathematics & Signal Fundamentals10 marks
  • Linear algebra: matrices, determinants, rank, eigenvalues/eigenvectors, solution of linear equations
  • Calculus: differentiation, integration, differential equations
  • Transforms: Fourier series, basics of Fourier and Laplace transforms
  • Signals and systems: signal types, operations, sampling theorem, convolution (conceptual), LTI systems
  • Frequency domain concepts: spectra, bandwidth, aliasing, basic filters
7. Electronic Devices, Analog Circuits & Network Theory20 marks

Electronic Devices

  • Semiconductor basics
  • PN junction diode and Zener diode
  • BJT and MOSFET: characteristics, biasing and switching
  • LED, photodiode, phototransistor
  • SCR and TRIAC (basic concepts)

Analog Circuits

  • Operational amplifiers: inverting and non-inverting amplifiers
  • Summing, integrator and differentiator circuits
  • Comparators and Schmitt trigger
  • Oscillators (RC, LC, crystal)
  • Rectifiers and filters

Network Theory

  • KVL and KCL
  • Mesh and nodal analysis
  • Network theorems
  • Resonance in RLC circuits
  • Frequency response of simple filters
8. Digital Electronics & Microprocessors20 marks

Digital Electronics

  • Number systems and conversions
  • Logic gates and Boolean algebra
  • Karnaugh maps
  • Combinational circuits
  • Sequential circuits: flip-flops, counters and shift registers

Microprocessors & Microcontrollers

  • 8085/8086 architecture and instruction set basics
  • Addressing modes, interrupts and timing
  • 8051 microcontroller fundamentals
  • Introduction to ARM architecture
  • Memory interfacing (conceptual)
9. Communication System20 marks
  • Analog communication: AM, FM, PM, modulation index, bandwidth, demodulation, noise
  • Digital communication: sampling, quantization, PCM, DPCM, DM
  • Digital modulation: ASK, FSK, PSK, QPSK
  • Error detection and correction (parity, CRC – basics)
  • SNR and BER concepts
  • Antennas: parameters and types
  • Wave propagation: ground, sky and space wave
  • Optical fibre communication and satellite communication
  • Wireless and mobile communication: cellular concepts, frequency reuse, handoff
  • Evolution of mobile networks: 2G to 5G
  • Multiple access techniques: FDMA, TDMA, CDMA, OFDMA
10. Computer Science & Networking Systems20 marks
  • OSI and TCP/IP models
  • IP addressing, subnetting and CIDR
  • Routing protocols: RIP and OSPF (basic concepts)
  • Switching techniques
  • Network devices: router, switch, hub
  • Wireless standards: Wi-Fi (802.11 family), Bluetooth, NFC
  • Programming fundamentals: logic building, data types, loops, functions
  • Object-oriented concepts
  • Data structures and algorithms (conceptual)
  • Operating systems: process, memory and file systems (basics)
11. Cyber Security & Legal Framework10 marks
  • Cyber threats: malware, phishing, ransomware
  • Network security: firewalls, IDS/IPS
  • Authentication and access control
  • Security protocols: HTTPS, SSL/TLS (basic concepts)
  • Cryptography: symmetric and asymmetric encryption, hashing, digital signatures
  • IT Act, 2000 and cyber offences relevant to policing
  • Introduction to post-quantum cryptography
12. IT System & e-Governance10 marks
  • Web application basics
  • Client–server architecture
  • Cloud computing fundamentals
  • Data centres, backup and disaster recovery
  • Government IT systems, interoperability and APIs (basic concepts)
13. Database Management System10 marks
  • Database system architecture and data models
  • Relational databases and SQL concepts
  • Database design and normalization
  • Integrity constraints
  • Transaction processing and concurrency control
  • Storage, indexing and file organization
  • Database security
14. IoT, Embedded System & Mechatronics10 marks
  • IoT architecture and M2M communication
  • Embedded and real-time systems
  • Surveillance and access control systems: CCTV, ANPR, RFID
  • Drone technology
  • Robotics fundamentals
  • Sensors and actuators
  • Basics of hydraulics and pneumatics
15. Artificial Intelligence, Data Science & Automation10 marks
  • Fundamentals of AI and its components
  • AI techniques and applications in engineering and government systems
  • Big data and analytics in law enforcement
  • Advantages, limitations and ethical concerns of AI
